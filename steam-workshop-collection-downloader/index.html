Steam Workshop Collection Downloader

A Steam Workshop collection bundles dozens or hundreds of mods into one list. Downloading every item by hand is painful — here are the two reliable ways to grab a whole collection.

Option 1: One by one via the browser tool

Open the collection page, right-click each mod and copy its link, then paste each link into our homepage downloader. Works fine for collections under ~10 items.

Option 2: SteamCMD batch script (recommended for big packs)

SteamCMD can download every item in a collection with a single command: workshop_download_item downloads the collection manifest, and most third-party wrapper scripts (like steamworkshopdownloader scripts) expand it into per-item downloads automatically.

Tips for huge modpacks

  • Run SteamCMD overnight for collections above 20 GB.
  • Check each mod's required DLC — some Workshop items depend on paid content.
  • RimWorld and Project Zomboid load mod load-order from your game, not the collection order.

FAQ

Can I download a collection with one click here?

Not yet — this tool downloads individual items. SteamCMD is the way to grab everything in bulk.

Does the collection order matter?

For downloading, no. For playing, your game's load order matters.
