How to Download Steam Workshop Mods

Downloading Steam Workshop mods without opening the Steam client is possible in two ways: a browser-based downloader like this one, or Valve's official SteamCMD tool. This guide covers both, so you can pick the method that fits your situation.

Method 1: Browser downloader (easiest)

  1. Copy the mod's Workshop URL from steamcommunity.com (it looks like steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=NUMBER).
  2. Paste the URL into the input field on our homepage and press Load.
  3. Review the preview: title, file size, last update, screenshots and description.
  4. Click Download ZIP. The file downloads straight to your device — no account, no ads.

Method 2: SteamCMD (official, best for bulk)

  1. Download SteamCMD from Valve's developer site and extract it.
  2. Run steamcmd.exe and wait for it to update.
  3. Log in anonymously: type login anonymous.
  4. Force the install dir: force_install_dir ./mods
  5. Download the item: workshop_download_item — for RimWorld mods the appId is 294100.
  6. Your mod ZIP appears in steamapps/workshop/content//.

Which method should I use?

Use the browser downloader for single mods — it's faster and requires no setup. Use SteamCMD when you need to download many mods at once, automate downloads on a server, or grab items larger than 500 MB.

FAQ

Do I need a Steam account?

No. Anonymous login works with SteamCMD, and the browser tool needs no account at all.

Where do downloaded mods go?

Your browser's default download folder. Move the ZIP into your game's mods folder to install.
