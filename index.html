Frequently asked questions

How do I download a Steam Workshop mod?

Copy the item's Workshop URL, paste it into the field above, press Load, then click Download ZIP. The file is fetched directly and saved to your device.

Can I download private Workshop items?

No. Only publicly visible Workshop items can be previewed and downloaded.

Why is my Workshop link not working?

The link must be a public sharedfiles/filedetails URL. Deleted, private, or region-restricted items cannot be fetched.

Is there a file size limit?

Downloads are recommended for items up to 500 MB. Larger items may queue or fail on the download service.

Can I download an entire Steam Workshop collection?

Not in one click yet. Each item must be downloaded individually by pasting its own Workshop link.
