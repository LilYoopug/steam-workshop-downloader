Garry's Mod Workshop Downloader

Garry's Mod's Workshop hosts hundreds of thousands of addons — maps, weapons, NPC packs, and more. This tool downloads any public addon straight to your device, no Steam client required.

How to download a GMod addon

  1. Copy the addon's Workshop URL (steamcommunity.com/app/4000/workshop).
  2. Paste it on the homepage, press Load, and check the preview.
  3. Click Download ZIP — the addon arrives as a .zip containing the addon's gma/content files.

Installing addons manually

Extract the ZIP into garrysmod/addons/. Each addon folder contains addon.json plus materials, models and lua directories. Restart GMod and the addon loads automatically. Legacy .gma files can also be placed in garrysmod/addons/ directly.

Large addon collections

Many GMod servers require dozens of addons at once. For bulk downloads use SteamCMD with app ID 4000, or paste addon links here one at a time — the preview shows file sizes before you commit.

FAQ

Do I need GMod installed to download?

No — files download to any device, even without Steam installed.

Why is my addon a .gma file?

That's GMod's archive format. Modern addons ship as folders; older ones as .gma. Both belong in garrysmod/addons/.
